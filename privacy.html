Aslamtu: Prayer Times & Azan

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Irfan Dagra

Aslamtu is a prayer-times and Azan app. It is free, contains no advertising, and was built to work entirely on your phone. This policy explains, plainly, what that means for your information.

We collect nothing

Aslamtu has no user accounts, no server of ours, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect, store, receive, sell, or share any personal data — because the app never sends any to us. There is nothing to sell and nothing to share.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings — chosen location, calculation method, adjustments, alert choices and voice — are stored only in the app on your device.
  • Prayer times are calculated on the device. Alerts are scheduled locally on the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not transmitted to us. If you search for a place by name, that lookup is handled by your phone's built-in map service (Apple or Google), and that provider's privacy policy applies to the lookup. You can also choose a city from the app's built-in list, in which case no lookup happens at all.

Audio

The Azan recordings are bundled inside the app and play on your device. Nothing about your listening leaves the device.

Children

Aslamtu is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
